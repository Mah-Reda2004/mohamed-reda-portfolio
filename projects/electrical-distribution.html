Complete Electrical Design

The project was developed as a complete electrical design package for a three-story residential villa in Saudi Arabia.

The design covers lighting, power distribution, AC power supply, cable routing, panel schedules, single-line diagrams, earthing, and low-current systems. The electrical layouts were developed based on the architectural plans, with the different systems documented clearly across the project floors.
