About Me

I’m an Electrical Power Engineer focused on Electrical Design, Technical Office, BIM/Revit, and Shop Drawings, with practical experience working on real engineering projects. My work covers electrical power distribution, lighting, electrical panels, cable routing, single-line diagrams, earthing systems, and low-current systems. I also have experience with systems such as Fire Alarm, CCTV, Data, Access Control, and Public Address. I use AutoCAD and Revit for developing electrical layouts, shop drawings, models, schedules, and technical documentation, with a focus on accuracy, clear presentation, and practical design. Through real project work and technical training, I continue to build my experience across different electrical systems and develop my skills in Electrical Design and Technical Office work.
